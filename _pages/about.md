I am a PhD student in Education Policy, Organization & Leadership (Human Resource Development) at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.
------------
- My research focuses on workplace AI–human interaction, AI-driven skill shifts, and the implications of emerging technologies for workplace learning, talent development, and post-hire competence formation.
- I am particularly interested in credential inflation and degree devaluation, and in how organizations and HRD professionals reconstruct signals of competence beyond formal degrees.
- My work sits at the intersection of education policy, human resource development, and the changing relationship between formal education and work.

I received my Ed.M. in Education Policy and Analysis (Comparative Education) from the Harvard Graduate School of Education (HGSE).
------------
- My master’s experience emphasized comparative and international perspectives on education policy, with a strong focus on equity, governance, and labor-market alignment.
- I developed a sustained interest in how education systems and organizations respond to structural changes in work, technology, and skill demand from a culturally responsive perspective.
