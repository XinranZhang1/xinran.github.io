As part of **HGSE core coursework**, conducted a policy analysis of primary education in Cambodia:  
- Investigated root causes of insufficient instructional time, including teacher shortages and urban-rural disparities.  
- Proposed evidence-based policy recommendations for improving teacher training, retention, and resource allocation.  
- Presented findings and policy recommendations directly to officials from the **Ministry of Education, Youth and Sport (MoEYS), Cambodia**.  
- Produced a comprehensive report detailing root causes, policy options, and recommendations.
