--- title: "Education Policy Analysis" excerpt: "Analyzed instructional time shortages and policy interventions in Cambodia's primary schools." collection: portfolio --- As part of **HGSE core coursework**, conducted a policy analysis of primary education in Cambodia: - Investigated root causes of insufficient instructional time, including teacher shortages, urban-rural disparities, and absenteeism. - Proposed evidence-based policy interventions to improve teacher training, retention, and equitable resource allocation. - Presented findings and policy recommendations directly to officials from the **Ministry of Education, Youth and Sport (MoEYS), Cambodia**. - Produced a comprehensive report detailing root causes, policy options, and recommendations.
